# Msomi Clan Management System - User Manual

Welcome to the Msomi Clan Management System. This manual provides instructions on how to effectively manage our clan's digital identity and records.

## 1. Dashboard Overview
The dashboard provides a high-level summary of clan statistics, including active members, recent contributions, and upcoming events.

## 2. Managing Members
- **Adding Members**: Navigate to "Members" > "Add Member". Ensure all required fields (Name, Gender, Region) are filled.
- **Family Tree**: Use the interactive "Family Tree" to visualize relationships. You can click on any member to view their full profile.
- **ID Cards**: From a member's profile page, click "Download ID Card" to generate a branded PDF.

## 3. Mila na Desturi (Customs & Traditions)
- **Adding Content**: Go to "Traditions" > "Add New". Use the rich text editor for descriptions.
- **Media Gallery**: Upload images and videos to the tradition records to preserve our cultural archives.

## 4. Financial Records
- **Recording Payments**: Go to "Contributions" > "Record Payment". Select the member and enter the amount and reference number.
- **Exporting Data**: Click "Export to Excel" on the contributions list to download a spreadsheet for offline auditing.

## 5. Communications
- **Announcements**: Announcements marked as 'Urgent' will automatically trigger SMS and Email alerts to all members.
- **Events**: Creating a new event will notify relevant members via email.
